# Playground (Dashboard)

- In-dashboard playground for trying `/v1/chat/completions`, seeing latency/tokens, and experimenting safely.
- Good for quick prompt/model checks without wiring a client.

Limitations & disclaimers
- Best-effort, advisory tool; not monitoring or an SLA.
- Uses your configured providers/keys; costs apply per provider pricing.

For general caveats and non-goals, see `docs/product/known-gaps-and-non-goals.md`.
