# Team & Roles (Dashboard + API)

- Manage organization members, invites, and basic roles from the dashboard and API.
- Supports listing members, sending invites, and removing invites/members.

Limitations & disclaimers
- Best-effort team management; not a full enterprise IAM/RBAC system.
- Roles/permissions are scoped to the app; pair Oxy with your identity provider for anything beyond them.

For general caveats and non-goals, see `docs/product/known-gaps-and-non-goals.md`.
